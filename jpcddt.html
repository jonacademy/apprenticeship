Before you log hours

Off-the-job learning must be new learning, relevant to the apprenticeship and completed within paid working hours. Record what you learned and how it develops your role, not simply the time spent. Agree suitability with your programme team or employer where needed.

Make the learning visible

A useful learning-log entry answers four questions

  1. What did I do?Name the resource and activity.
  2. What was new?Capture the specific knowledge or skill gained.
  3. How is it relevant?Connect it to your role and apprenticeship.
  4. What will I do differently?Describe an application, output or next step.

External content can change. Check access, relevance and accuracy before use.
